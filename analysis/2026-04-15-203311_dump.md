### SameSite Cookie Analysis — Key Exploit Limiter
- Session cookies (`session=`, `login_id=`, `1XJgM5twoV0e8IhkwW0BQuDwzkz=`) have NO `SameSite` attribute
- Chrome 80+ / Firefox 79+: cookies without SameSite default to `SameSite=Lax`
- `SameSite=Lax` means cookies ARE sent on top-level navigation but NOT on cross-site XHR/fetch
- → In modern browsers, `fetch('https://app-us.smtp2go.com/api/', {credentials:'include'})` from `http://insights.hotjar.com` would NOT include session cookies
- → Attack feasible on: (a) pre-2020 browsers, (b) browsers that explicitly received `SameSite=None` cookies, (c) future regression
- Cookies also lack `Secure` flag — further weakens cookie security posture

### OPTIONS Preflight Weakness
- OPTIONS returns `ACAO: http://insights.hotjar.com` but NO `Access-Control-Allow-Methods`
- Preflighted requests (POST, custom headers) would be blocked even from the trusted origin
- GET simple requests do NOT require preflight → the readable endpoints above are all GET
