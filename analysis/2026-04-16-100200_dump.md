### SSRF vs Normal Webhook — Core Distinction
- Normal: smtp2go POSTs event data TO your server (you're the receiver)
- SSRF: smtp2go fetches FROM a URL you supply (smtp2go is the HTTP client to your target)
- Test button is SSRF because the fetch happens immediately on your request, not on an email event
- Evidence of server-side fetch: source IP in interactsh will be smtp2go's server, not your browser
