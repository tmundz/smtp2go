### [CONFIRMED] WCD: Cloudflare IS Present (was invisible in captures)

Cloudflare is fronting app-us.smtp2go.com:
- Suffix paths without session → 404 from origin (CF passes through, `cf-cache-status: DYNAMIC`)
- Suffix paths with session → **403 from CF WAF** (error code 1010 — WAF/bot protection rule)
- Normal authenticated pages → 200 from gunicorn (CF acts as pass-through, no CF headers in response)

**Why CF was invisible in original captures**: Caido was intercepting HTTPS traffic locally.
The CF-RAY and CF headers appear in direct connections, not through Caido's intercepted path.
(Or: CF is configured to strip its own headers on authenticated responses.)

**WCD verdict**:
- Unauthenticated suffix paths → 404 (nothing sensitive to cache)
- Authenticated suffix paths → CF WAF blocks (403)
- **WCD is NOT currently exploitable** — CF WAF actively prevents suffix-based WCD
- The structural risk (no Cache-Control headers) remains if CF WAF rules change
